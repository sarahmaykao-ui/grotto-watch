How this works and what it can't promise

  • Twice a day an automated check searches venue websites, ticket sellers (Fever, Eventbrite and similar) and London listings sites, then updates this page.
  • "Going fast" means the venue or ticket seller said so, or some dates or ticket types showed as sold out. "Sold out" means every date looked gone at the last check. Returns and extra dates do get released, so it's worth re-checking the official page.
  • Many booking sites only show which slots are full once you click into their calendar. This page can't see those, so a slot you want may already be gone.
  • Toddler notes come from what the venue says (age limits, session lengths, whether babies need tickets). They're not a safety or suitability check. Use your own judgement about crowds, noise, dark trails and late starts.
  • Free events can be ticketed or first come, first served. Check the card and the venue page.
  • Nothing here is a recommendation or an endorsement, and we're not responsible for a venue's event, its prices, its cancellations or your booking.
  • Spotted a mistake or a missing grotto? Tell whoever shared this page with you.
